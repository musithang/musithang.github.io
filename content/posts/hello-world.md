You somehow found your way here. Either you were looking for something else, or my SEO is better than I thought. Probably the former.

This is a personal technical archive. Not a blog in the traditional sense, because "blog" implies a certain regularity and a certain optimism about readership. This is more of a filing cabinet. Things get filed here when I'm tired of explaining them twice.

## What goes in here

Whatever I'm working on. Background processes for a development. The occasional philosophical tangent. A project log that no one asked for. The general idea is to document the *why* and the *how*, not just the *what*. The what is usually in the README. The why is usually lost in a Slack thread or a forgotten terminal tab.

## The catalog system

Every entry gets a catalog number. This is not a gimmick. Well, it's *a* gimmick, but it's also a system. The numbers are assigned sequentially, and each entry carries a barcode generated from its title and date. It looks like a y2k brutalist artifact because it is. The aesthetic is deliberate: retro-futuristic, slightly broken, honest about its own construction.

## The rules

- Ship before it's perfect.
- If it builds without errors, ship it.
- Regret is a tomorrow problem.
