Running your own workspace is cheaper than a subscription and more expensive than your free time. Worth it. Mostly.

## The math

Cloud AI services cost roughly a kidney per month if you use them seriously. A used workstation with a decent GPU costs about three months of that subscription. The math is not complicated.

What the math doesn't account for is the weekend you'll spend getting CUDA drivers to cooperate with your kernel version.

## The honest review

It works about 90% of the time. The other 10% involves restarting containers, clearing caches, or muttering things at configuration files that no one should hear.

Would I recommend it? If you enjoy tinkering, absolutely. If you want reliability, stick with the cloud.
